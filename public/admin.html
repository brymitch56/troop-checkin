Automatic roster sync

A weekly job fetches the member export from Trail Life Connect and stages it here as a pending import. It never commits on its own — you approve or discard the diff below. Only one pending import exists at a time; a newer fetch replaces the old one.

Trail Life Connect credentials

Saved credentials live on the check-in server with the rest of the troop's protected data and are never shown again here — to change the password, just type a new one (leave it blank to keep the current one when changing the email). They're used for exactly one things: downloading the member export, and — only if you enable it below — marking attendance on Trail Life Connect events.

⚠ Multiple roles? Trail Life Connect signs in under whichever role the account used last, and the export only works from a role with member-list access (e.g. Troopmaster). If this account has several roles, log into TLC in a browser and switch to the permitted role before relying on the sync — if a fetch fails or comes back suspiciously small, check the active role first.

TLC attendance write-back

When enabled, everyone who signs out at the kiosk (or is closed out by an admin / SMS pickup confirmation) is marked Attended on the matching Trail Life Connect calendar event (matched automatically through the iCal feed). The sign-out cart asks per youth whether they completed all planned requirements (default yes): checked pushes attendance with advancement credit for the event's activity plans; unchecked pushes attendance without advancement for that youth only — exactly like TLC's own Track Attendance page with the advancement box on or off. The app only ever marks attendance; it never un-marks anyone on TLC. Per-event overrides live in the event editor on the Events tab.
