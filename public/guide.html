Part 1 · The kiosk (door staff)

Signing in to the kiosk

  1. Open the app on a phone or tablet on the meeting-place Wi-Fi (install it once with the browser's Add to Home Screen so it launches like an app).
  2. Tap your name on the staff list.
  3. Door staff enter their PIN; admins without a PIN type their password. (An admin who has been given a PIN uses the PIN.)

Your name shows in the top-right pill; tap it to sign out. The kiosk signs itself out after ~20 minutes of inactivity — but never while a cart is in progress or while offline.

Every check-in records which staff member ran it, so each person should use their own account.

Choosing the event

Sign-ins always attach to an event. The kiosk picks the event automatically when exactly one is happening right now; otherwise tap the event pill (top of the screen) to choose. Events come from the troop calendar (synced nightly) — you can also create one on the spot from the same picker.

  • Events are listed soonest-first; past events are tucked behind a "Show past events" toggle.
  • Multi-day events (campouts) stay current through their final day — sign-outs days after the sign-in attach to the right event automatically.
  • Sign-out never needs the event: it follows the person's open sign-in.

Adding people: scan, search, visitor

Three ways to put someone in the cart:

  • Badge scan. A Bluetooth scanner works any time — just scan, even mid-typing (the kiosk recognizes scanner bursts and keeps typed text clean). Or tap 📷 Scan badge to use the camera.
  • Name search. Type 2+ letters of a first name, last name, or nickname.
  • + Visitor. A youth or adult who isn't on the roster. Youth visitors take a guardian name/phone, and that guardian becomes their authorized signer immediately.

Badge enrollment & reprints

The first scan of a new badge whose member number matches the roster asks "Link badge?" — confirm and it's enrolled for good. A reprinted badge (same member, new code) triggers the same prompt; confirming re-links it. A badge that matches nothing gets a friendly "not recognized" message — use search instead.

The cart & emergency phones

People you add stack up in the cart so one guardian signature can cover several kids. The kiosk sets the direction automatically: someone currently on site goes OUT, everyone else goes IN — and one cart is all-IN or all-OUT (finish one direction before starting the other).

  • Each youth signing IN shows an emergency phone field, pre-filled from last time (or the primary guardian's mobile). Confirm or update it — it's snapshotted onto the record.
  • Adults are only tracked at events marked "Track adult attendance"; at youth-only meetings the kiosk politely refuses to add them.
  • Tap ✕ on a row to remove it; Clear empties the cart.

Signing youth in and out

  1. Tap Sign IN (n) / Sign OUT (n).
  2. Pick who is signing — the list is the union of authorized adults for everyone in the cart. A yellow note flags an adult who isn't on the list for every youth in the cart.
  3. "Other adult…" lets any adult sign with their name typed in — use for one-off situations, with staff judgment.
  4. The signer draws their signature and you save. Done.

If the chosen signer isn't authorized for someone in the cart, the kiosk asks for a staff override confirmation. Overrides are allowed — you're in charge at the door — but each one is flagged and appears in the admin's override report.

Adult-only carts skip the signer and signature entirely — arrivals/departures are just recorded.
Race protection: if another station beat you to it ("already signed in/out"), the kiosk tells you and clears the cart — nothing is double-recorded.

Membership-expiration warnings

If a youth's Trail Life membership has expired or expires within 30 days, their cart row shows an orange ⚠ Membership expiring tag, and the sign modal repeats it with the date: "Membership expires Sep 16 — renewal due."

The warning never blocks check-in. It's a nudge for the family and leadership — sign the youth in normally and mention the renewal to the parent at pickup.

Admins can see everyone expiring soon under Reports → Membership renewals (see Part 2).

The "On site" screen & texting guardians

The On site: n pill shows the live headcount; tap it for the full list grouped by event, filterable by patrol. From this screen (once SMS is set up):

  • 🔔 Text guardians — one message per family listing all their youth still on site ("please pick up"). A guardian with three kids on site gets one text, not three. The results screen lists exactly who was not contacted and why (no consent, no mobile, STOPped) so you can phone those families instead.
  • ✉️ Message — a custom broadcast ("bus is 20 minutes late"): to guardians of everyone still on site, or to everyone who attended the current event (even if already picked up).
Texts go only to guardians whose family signed the SMS consent form and was opted in by an admin. Nobody else can be texted from the kiosk, ever.

Station mode (per-patrol devices)

Tap the station pill (top bar) to cycle a device through patrol scopes: "All patrols" → each patrol in turn. A patrol-scoped station narrows name search and the on-site view to that patrol — handy when each patrol leader carries a sign-out phone. Badge scans always work regardless of scope. The setting sticks per device.

Working offline

The kiosk keeps working when the Wi-Fi or server drops (requires HTTPS or an installed PWA; it caches the roster at every login):

  • Search, badge lookup, and guardian lists come from the last-saved roster snapshot — including membership-expiry warnings.
  • Sign-ins/outs are saved locally with a ⏳ queued pill and sync automatically when the connection returns (usually within 20 seconds).
  • If a queued record is rejected on sync (e.g. another station already signed that youth out), it moves to a ⚠ conflicts pill — tap it to review and clear. The server's version of events is the truth.
Before a campout at a no-signal site: open the app once on Wi-Fi while signed in — that refreshes the offline roster.

Part 2 · Admin

Admin sign-in & dashboard

Open /admin.html (link at the bottom of the kiosk). Admins sign in with their password (or their PIN if one is set). The Dashboard shows count cards — active youth/adults, visitors, on site now, events, transactions, and renewals due ≤30 days (orange when non-zero) — plus tools (backup now, iCal sync, quick CSVs) and the live "still on site" list with an Admin close button for stragglers (see Corrections).

People & the person editor

People tab: search by name or member number, filter youth/adults and active/inactive/visitor. Click a row to open the person editor.

  • Edit names, nickname, patrol/level (youth) or role (adults), phones, birthdate, membership expires, email, status, notes.
  • Photos (youth): add a photo for visual confirmation at pickup — it appears in the kiosk sign modal.
  • Merge: a visitor youth who later joins the troop can be merged into their roster record — attendance history moves with them.

Field locks — your edits beat the roster file

Any import-managed field you edit by hand gets a 🔒 and is never overwritten by future roster imports. The editor lists the locked fields; "let imports manage these again" unlocks them all.

Guardians, pickup authorization & consent forms

Each youth's editor lists their guardians/authorized adults with: relationship, Authorized (may sign this youth in/out), Primary (first choice for texts), SMS consent state, and source (import vs manual).

  • Roster imports suggest guardian links automatically (family email, then same last name or address). Your manual edits are permanent — imports never change or duplicate them.
  • Import-created links can't be deleted (they'd come back next import) — mark them Not authorized instead.
  • Consent-form designees: grandparents, neighbors — any adult a family authorizes in writing can be added as a brand-new person and linked in one step. They appear in the kiosk signer picker immediately.
  • ＋ Add guardian / consent (one or many youth) opens the family dialog: pick or create the adult, tick every youth it applies to, set relationship/primary, and (optionally) attach the signed consent form and opt in — all in one save.
SMS opt-in is strict: a guardian can only be opted in with a stored signed consent form attached to that youth↔guardian pair. One uploaded family form can cover several pairs. Opt-in can be revoked any time; a guardian texting STOP is honored automatically.

SMS: alerts, broadcasts & the message log

  • Pickup alerts: a background sweep (every 5 min) texts the primary opted-in guardian of any youth still on site past the event's end + grace period (default 30 min, adjustable per event). One text per guardian covers all their youth; replying Y signs them all out (recorded as an SMS confirmation with the guardian as signer).
  • Broadcasts from the kiosk (see Part 1) are logged and repeatable; replies to a broadcast do not trigger sign-outs.
  • Messages tab: every SMS in and out — alerts, broadcasts, guardian replies, delivery status — refreshing every 15 s while open. You can reply directly to a guardian from here (blocked if they've texted STOP).
  • Compliance export: every youth↔guardian pair with its consent state and form → sms-consent.csv.

Events & calendar sync

  • Events sync nightly from the troop calendar feed (or on demand: Dashboard → Sync iCal now). Calendar times re-assert on each sync; other edits stick.
  • Edit title/location/times, Track adult attendance (off = youth-only, adults refused at the kiosk), and the SMS reminder delay.
  • An event that disappears from the calendar feed is deleted if unused, or kept and flagged "gone from feed" if it has attendance records.
  • Events with transactions can't be deleted — history is permanent.

Transactions, voids & corrections

Transactions tab: every sign-in/out with people, event, signer, staff, flags (override, voided, admin) — click one for details including the signature image. Filter by event; export CSV.

Fixing mistakes — nothing is ever deleted

  • Void a transaction: a correcting record is added and linked. Voiding a sign-IN takes the youth off site; voiding a sign-OUT puts them back on site.
  • Admin close (Dashboard): closes a lingering open sign-in without a guardian signature — e.g. a family that walked out without signing. Recorded as an admin action under your name.

Reports, renewals & CSV exports

  • Attendance summary: pick a date range, event, and/or one person → events attended, sign-in counts, first/last seen. Records are kept forever, so a 3-year lookback is just a wider range. Detail + summary CSVs.
  • Membership renewals: everyone (youth and registered adults) whose membership has expired or expires within 30/60/90 days, most-urgent first — with a CSV for chasing renewals. Already-lapsed members are listed too.
  • SMS notification log: every pickup alert with its status (sent, replied-Y, failed).
  • Dashboard quick CSVs: open sign-ins, overrides, visitors.

Roster import

  1. Export the member list from Trail Life Connect (.xlsx).
  2. Import tab → choose the file → Preview: see exactly who would be added, updated (and which fields), or deactivated. Nothing changes yet.
  3. Commit when the preview looks right.
  • Re-import as often as you like — it's idempotent. Blank cells never wipe existing data; your hand-edited (🔒) fields and guardian edits are never touched.
  • People missing from the new file are marked inactive (never deleted) — scoped to the classes in the file, so a youth-only export leaves adults alone. Visitors are never deactivated. Returning members reactivate automatically.
  • Membership expiration dates come in from the export's "Membership Exp." column automatically.
  • Every upload is archived, and the import log shows who imported what, when, with counts.

Automatic roster sync

The Import tab can also fetch the export from Trail Life Connect for you — on a weekly schedule (if the server admin enabled the timer) or via Sync now. A fetch never changes anything by itself: it stages a pending import showing when it was fetched, the row count, and the full add/update/deactivate diff, with Approve / Discard buttons. Approving runs exactly the same commit path as a manual upload; only one pending import exists at a time (a newer fetch replaces the old one and says so). The panel shows the last run's status, so a broken sync is visible instead of silent. The Trail Life Connect login it uses can be entered and updated right in the panel ("Trail Life Connect credentials") — the password is stored on the check-in server, used only to download the export, and never displayed again. One caution: if that account has multiple Trail Life Connect roles, TLC signs in under whichever role was used last, and the export only works from a role with member-list access (like Troopmaster) — if a sync fails or looks too small, check the account's active role first.

Staff accounts

  • Door staff sign in with a short PIN; admins with a password. Giving an admin a PIN makes the PIN their login (handy for door duty); clearing it restores the password.
  • Create, rename, deactivate, reactivate — deactivation signs them out everywhere instantly. Guard rails: you can't deactivate yourself or the last active admin.

Backups

Nightly automatic backups (database snapshot + signatures), kept 14 deep, plus an optional encrypted off-site push. Back up now is on the Dashboard — use it before anything risky. Restore instructions live with the server documentation.

Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat it means / what to do
"Already signed in/out" when signing the cartAnother station handled them first. The cart clears; re-check the On site list.
Badge scan says "not recognized"Badge isn't linked and its member number isn't on the roster — use name search, or import the newest roster.
⏳ pill with a numberRecords saved offline, waiting to sync. They'll go automatically when the connection returns.
⚠ pillAn offline record was rejected on sync (usually a race with another station). Tap it, read, clear.
Adult can't be added to the cartThis event doesn't track adults. That's per-event: Admin → Events → Track adult attendance.
Guardian didn't get the pickup textCheck the kiosk's "not contacted" list or Admin → Messages: no consent/opt-in, no mobile number, or they texted STOP.
Orange membership tag on a youthRenewal due within 30 days (or lapsed). Check-in is unaffected; mention it to the family.
App looks stale after an updateReload twice (the app shell is cached for offline use; the second reload activates the new version).
Something looks wrong server-sideContact the troop's app administrator — the server keeps full logs, and nightly backups mean nothing is lost.
